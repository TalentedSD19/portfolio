ABOUT

The plumbing matters as much as the model.

I'm a dual-degree computer science student and AI engineer who likes shipping things that work end to end, not just in a notebook. Over the last two years I've built a multi-agent data science platform that writes, runs, and debugs its own analysis code, a smart glove that turns sign language into speech, a vision system that narrates the world for people who can't see it, and a RAG pipeline that reads codebases for vulnerabilities at better than 95% accuracy.

Two of those became peer-reviewed papers at Springer conferences. Two became hackathon wins. All of them taught me the same lesson: the hard part of applied AI is rarely the model itself, it's the sensors, latency budgets, sandboxes, and interfaces that carry a prediction to someone who actually needs it.

Right now I split my time between a B.Tech in CSE (AI & ML) at Heritage Institute of Technology and a BSc in Data Science at IIT Madras, while building agentic systems with LangGraph, MCP, RAG, and computer vision.

DataScientistOS
A team of agents that does the analysis for you
View on GitHub ↗

A multi-agent, LLM-based data science platform inspired by Google's DS-STAR. LangGraph orchestrates iterative planning, routing, backtracking, and automated debugging of generated code, so the system can go from a raw dataset to exploratory data analysis, generated code, verified results, and a written report on its own. Generated code runs inside a Docker sandbox with CPU and memory limits and network isolation; tools are exposed over MCP behind a FastAPI + React interface.

Kontho
A glove that speaks sign language out loud
Watch demo ↗

Flex sensors and a gyroscope on an ESP32 capture ASL hand gestures and stream them over Bluetooth to a mobile app, where sensor-signal feature engineering feeds a stacked CatBoost ensemble — layered with SVM, Random Forest, and XGBoost over 5,000+ samples at 99.61% accuracy — that classifies the sign, and Llama 3 (via the Groq API, through LangChain) cleans up the grammar before it's spoken aloud. Hosted on AWS EC2, with bidirectional voice/text communication so it works back the other way too.

IN: flex-sensor + gyroscope reading⟶OUT: spoken, grammar-corrected English
ESP32CatBoostXGBoostLangChainGroq · Llama 3FlaskAWS EC2
